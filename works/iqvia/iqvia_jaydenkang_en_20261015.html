AI transformation is 70% about people, only 10% about AI

How to allocate effort for a successful field-service AI transformation

70%

Focus on people and process

  • Lead with a clear vision and a compelling case that energizes people
  • Bring service teams in through co-creation and fast iteration
  • Redefine processes and roles to sharpen execution
  • Align culture with service strategy and KPIs
  • Upskill teams and build the capabilities they need
10%

Focus on AI

  • Apply field-service machine learning, predictive analytics and generative AI
20%

Focus on data and technology

  • Put robust technology in the hands of frontline teams
  • Integrate field and service systems to automate end to end

Source: BCG, “AI Fuels Field Service Transformation. Change Management Provides the Spark,” January 2026

Research shows how meetings change once agents join

Within the team

P&G × Harvard field experiment
776 professionals

Individuals with AI matched two-person teams without AI, and reported more positive emotions

What changesMeetings move from building drafts to judging them together

Between companies

MIT AI negotiation competition
180,000+ agent-to-agent negotiations

Warm, friendly agents consistently won better deals than ruthless ones

What changesAgents run the first rounds; people meet to build trust and close

In open markets

Microsoft Research simulation
100 buyer agents, 300 seller agents

Agents favored the first offer they saw, and some could be steered by misleading signals

What changesPeople keep the final call; agents work under supervision

Sources: Dell’Acqua et al., “The Cybernetic Teammate,” NBER Working Paper 33641, 2025; Vaccaro et al., “Advancing AI Negotiations,” PNAS, 2026; Microsoft Research, “Magentic Marketplace,” 2025
